A gathered space for presence, love, and awareness

Kindled Earth is a gathered space — held together, moment to moment, by whoever shows up. There's no account to create and nothing to download. Choose an intention, choose the region you're joining from, and you become part of a space other people are holding too, awake and present, right where they are. The live map fills in as more people arrive, so a private moment becomes something visibly shared.

Why a live world map

Most meditation happens alone, in private. Kindled Earth adds a second layer of awareness — a simple, honest window into who else is holding this same space with you right now, and roughly where in the world they are. It's a quiet way to feel gathered, rather than alone, in a practice that's usually solitary.

Send love, lift the world, find stillness

Each intention on this page — lifting the planet's energy, sending love to the world, holding space for peace, practicing gratitude, or sending healing to people who are struggling — opens its own gathered room, with its own live counter and map, so you can join whichever kind of space-holding matches how you want to spend the next few minutes.

Free, anonymous, always open

Kindled Earth doesn't ask for your name, your exact location, or a login. It's free, it runs in your browser, and the space is always open — there's no scheduled start time to wait for. Gathering, holding space, and sending love to the world are all practices you can begin the moment you feel called to.
