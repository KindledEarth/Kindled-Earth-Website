A free, live space for global meditation

Kindled Earth is a place to meditate and send love around the world alongside other people, in real time. There's no account to create and nothing to download — pick an intention, choose the region you're joining from, and you're in a session with everyone else doing the same thing at that moment, anywhere on Earth. The live map fills in as more people join, so a solo sit turns into something visibly shared.

Why a live world map

Most meditation apps are private by design — just you and a timer. Kindled Earth adds a second layer: a simple, honest view of who else is meditating with you right now, and roughly where in the world they are. It's a small way to feel less alone in a practice that's usually solitary.

Send love, lift the world, find stillness

Each intention on this page — lifting the planet's energy, sending love to the world, holding space for peace, practicing gratitude, or sending healing to people who are struggling — is its own live room with its own counter and its own map, so you can join whichever one matches how you want to spend the next few minutes.

Free, anonymous, always open

Kindled Earth doesn't ask for your name, your exact location, or a login. It's free to use, runs entirely in your browser, and is open any time — there's no scheduled start time to wait for. Meditation and sending love to the world are both practices you can begin the moment you feel like it.
